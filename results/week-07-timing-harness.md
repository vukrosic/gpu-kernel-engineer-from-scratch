# Week 07 Timing Harness

## What Was Built

Write the note for your timing harness. Describe the helper you used, the number
of repeats, and whether you included warmup. If you compared two inputs, say
what changed.

## Correctness Check

Record that the same function and inputs were used across repeats, and note any
sanity check you used to make sure the harness itself was not changing the work.

## Benchmark Or Observation

Paste the sample timings, then summarize the median, the mean, and one reason
they differed. Keep the lesson focused on variability and measurement discipline.

## Lesson Learned

Explain why one timing is not enough and why repeated measurements are a better
fit for GPU or CPU performance work.

## Limitation Or Next Step

Name one thing you would improve in the harness if you reused it in later
weeks.

## Write Down

- Why is one timing result not enough?
- Why do repeats help?
- What is the point of warmup?
- When would you trust the median more than the mean?
