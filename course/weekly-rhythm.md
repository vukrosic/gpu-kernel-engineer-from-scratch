# Weekly Rhythm

Each flagship video contains the week's concept, implementation, benchmark, and
assignment plan. Students then use the rest of the week to build.

| Day | Learner Task | Creator Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | Watch the lesson and write a 5-sentence summary. | Publish flagship video and assignment. |
| Day 2 | Reproduce the starter code or reference implementation. | Post a short concept clip. |
| Day 3 | Implement the kernel or experiment. | Answer common debugging questions. |
| Day 4 | Add correctness tests against a baseline. | Share a testing tip or failure case. |
| Day 5 | Benchmark and record results. | Share benchmark interpretation. |
| Day 6 | Catch up, debug, simplify, or ask for help. | Host office-hours style post or livestream. |
| Day 7 | Rest, write portfolio notes, and prep for next week. | Publish recap and preview. |

## Weekly Video Shape

Recommended flagship structure:

1. Hook: why this kernel or concept matters for AI systems.
2. Mental model: the idea before the code.
3. Implementation: build the kernel or reference.
4. Correctness: compare against CPU, NumPy, or PyTorch.
5. Benchmark: measure and interpret.
6. Assignment: Minimum, Standard, and Stretch.
7. Portfolio note: what to publish.

## Keeping It Human

You are expected to miss days. The course is designed for recovery:

- Day 6 is a catch-up day.
- Day 7 is lighter.
- Week 4 of each month is a checkpoint.
- Quarterly reset weeks protect the long-term arc.
